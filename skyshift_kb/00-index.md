## About this knowledge base

This knowledge base is the official, consolidated body of public information about **SkyShift**, known in Hebrew as **"עולים לאוויר"** ("Olim La'Avir", "Taking to the Air"). It is intended for participants, prospective participants, partners and journalists.

## Current program status (important)

SkyShift is currently in its **demonstration phase**. The SkyShift platform (website, participant workspace and manager workspace) is live as a demonstration environment. The vehicles, flights and lottery shown in the platform **illustrate a planned future trial**. As of the date of this document:

- There is **no active passenger flight service**.
- There is **no active lottery draw**.
- No SkyShift vehicle has received an airworthiness certificate from any aviation authority.
- The vehicle images in the catalog are **illustrations**: familiar road cars with added propellers. The road cars shown do not fly.

Every document in this knowledge base should be read in light of this status.

## Key facts at a glance

- **Name:** SkyShift (Hebrew: עולים לאוויר).
- **Founded:** March 2025, central Israel.
- **Purpose:** to explore a future in which road congestion alone does not decide how people travel, by combining the familiarity of a car with the possibilities of movement in the sky.
- **Tagline:** "A car on the road. Options in the sky."
- **Trial length:** exactly one month per vehicle assignment.
- **Monthly target:** average daily trips declared at registration × 30.
- **Lottery eligibility threshold:** 80% of the monthly target, counted within the active trial month.
- **Lottery prize (planned):** the experimental vehicle, at no cost, according to the trial terms.
- **Vehicle catalog:** 22 models.
- **Roles in the platform:** Participant (User) and Manager.
- **Support contact:** support@skyshift.demo
