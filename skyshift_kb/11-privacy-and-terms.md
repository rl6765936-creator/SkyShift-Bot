# Privacy Policy and Terms of Participation

**Document ID:** SKS-KB-011
**Version:** 1.0
**Owner:** Legal
**Last updated:** 30 October 2026

---

> This document is a summary of SkyShift's privacy practices and participation terms for the demonstration phase and the planned pilot. It is not legal advice. In case of any question, the full binding terms published at the time of participation prevail.

## Part A: Privacy Policy

### A.1 What information we collect

| Information | Why we collect it |
|---|---|
| Full name | To identify you and address you correctly |
| Email address | To sign you in and contact you |
| Password | Stored only as a secure hash; we never see or store the password itself |
| Average daily trips | To calculate your personal monthly target |
| Gender (optional) | Only to address you correctly in Hebrew |
| Vehicle assignments | To run your trial month |
| Flight records (date and time, and the server time the record was created) | To calculate progress and eligibility, and to check integrity |
| Technical logs (including a request identifier) | To keep the system secure and investigate problems |

In the pilot stage, flight data from the vehicle will also be collected to verify flights and for safety purposes.

### A.2 What we do not do

- We do **not** sell personal information.
- We do **not** use your information for advertising by third parties.
- We do **not** ask for or store your password in readable form.
- We do **not** show your personal data to other participants.

### A.3 Who can see your information

- **You:** your profile, vehicle, flights and dashboard.
- **SkyShift Managers:** participant lists, activity, flights and assignments, for running the trial and ensuring fairness.
- **The independent observer of the draw:** anonymous identifiers of eligible participants only.
- **Service providers:** hosting and database providers that store the data on SkyShift's behalf, under appropriate obligations.
- **Authorities:** when required by law, or in connection with safety investigations.

### A.4 How we protect your information

- Encrypted connections (HTTPS) between your browser and our servers.
- Passwords stored as secure hashes.
- Access to management functions restricted to authorized Managers and protected on the server.
- Secret keys stored only in protected environment settings.
- Sessions that expire automatically.

### A.5 How long we keep information

Assignment and flight history is kept for as long as needed for the trial, the draw, safety and research purposes, and as required by law. History is not deleted when a vehicle is removed from the fleet, because it is part of the trial record.

### A.6 Your rights

You may request to see the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or, where the law allows, to delete it. Requests can be sent to **privacy@skyshift.demo**. Some information may need to be kept even after a deletion request, for example for safety or legal reasons.

### A.7 Information for research

SkyShift may use **anonymous, aggregated** information (for example, average completion percentages or numbers of flights) for research and publications about personal air mobility. Such publications will not identify any individual participant.

## Part B: Terms of Participation

### B.1 Status of the program

The SkyShift platform currently operates as a **demonstration environment**. Vehicles, flights and the lottery shown in it illustrate a future trial. There is currently **no** flight service and **no** active lottery. Participation in the demonstration does not create any right to a vehicle, a flight or a prize.

### B.2 Eligibility to participate

- Participants must be aged 18 or over.
- Each person may hold **one** account.
- The information you provide at registration must be true.

### B.3 Participant obligations

As a participant, you agree to:

1. Declare your average daily trips honestly.
2. Record only flights that actually took place, with the correct date and time.
3. Keep your password confidential and not share your account.
4. Follow every safety instruction from SkyShift staff and safety pilots (in the pilot stage).
5. Not attempt to bypass, overload or interfere with the platform.
6. Not attempt to access other participants' data or management functions.

### B.4 Vehicle assignment

- A vehicle is assigned for exactly one month from the moment of selection.
- A participant may hold only one active assignment at a time.
- In the pilot stage, the vehicle remains the property of SkyShift during the trial, unless awarded as a prize under the lottery regulations.

### B.5 Flights (pilot stage)

- Flights are carried out only by a SkyShift safety pilot, only between approved SkyPads, and only when all safety conditions are met.
- SkyShift may cancel or postpone any flight for safety, weather, technical or regulatory reasons, without the cancellation counting against the participant.

### B.6 Integrity

False information, false flight records or attempts to manipulate the platform may lead to removal from the trial and from the draw.

### B.7 Changes

SkyShift may update these terms. Material changes will be published at least 30 days before they take effect, and will not apply retroactively to a trial month already in progress in a way that harms the participant.

### B.8 Contact

Questions about privacy: **privacy@skyshift.demo**. Questions about participation: **support@skyshift.demo**.
