## About SkyShift

**What is SkyShift?**
SkyShift (Hebrew: "עולים לאוויר") is an Israeli venture exploring personal air mobility. It runs a trial program in which participants receive a vehicle for one month, record their flights, and can qualify for a lottery for the experimental vehicle.

**When was SkyShift founded?**
In March 2025, in central Israel.

**Who founded SkyShift?**
Yonatan Peretz (CEO), a transport planner, and Eliav Ben-Ami (CTO), a software architect. They were joined by Dr. Avraham Shachar, Chief Safety and Airworthiness Officer, and Moshe Levinger, Head of Operations and Participant Experience.

**Why was SkyShift founded?**
To give people back the time they lose in traffic, and to test, responsibly and measurably, a future in which the road is not the only way forward.

**What does the name mean?**
"SkyShift" means shifting part of daily travel to the sky. The Hebrew name, "עולים לאוויר", means "taking to the air".

**What is SkyShift's tagline?**
"A car on the road. Options in the sky."

**Is SkyShift a government project?**
No. SkyShift is an independent venture. It is not part of Israel's National Drone Initiative, although it follows regulatory developments in Israel closely.

## Current status

**Can I fly with SkyShift today?**
No. SkyShift is in its demonstration phase. There is no active flight service.

**Is the lottery active?**
No. The draw is planned for the pilot stage. No prize is awarded during the demonstration phase.

**So what can I do today?**
You can register in the SkyShift platform, select a vehicle, record flights and follow the dashboard, as a demonstration of how the trial will work.

**When will the pilot start?**
A closed pilot is planned for 2027, subject to regulatory approvals. No exact date has been published.

## The trial

**How long is the trial?**
Exactly one month from the moment you select a vehicle, including the first and last moments.

**How is my target calculated?**
Your average daily trips (declared at registration) × 30. For example, 4 trips a day gives a target of 120.

**Can I hold two vehicles?**
No. One active vehicle per participant.

**What if someone else selects the vehicle I wanted at the same moment?**
Only one of you will get it. The other will see a message that the vehicle is no longer available and can choose another.

**Can I record a flight for tomorrow?**
No. Only flights that already happened, with a date and time not in the future.

**Can I record a flight from before my trial started?**
No. Only flights within your trial month count.

**What if I record the same flight twice?**
A flight with exactly the same date and time is rejected and is not counted twice.

**Do flights from a previous trial month count?**
No. Only flights within the current active trial month count.

**Can I change my average daily trips after registering?**
Not in the platform at this stage. Contact support@skyshift.demo.

**I forgot my password. What do I do?**
Password reset is not yet available in the platform. Contact support.

## The lottery

**How do I enter the draw?**
There is no separate sign-up. You enter automatically by completing at least 80% of your monthly target within your trial month.

**How many flights do I need?**
80% of (average daily trips × 30). For example: 1 trip a day → 24 flights; 2 → 48; 3 → 72; 4 → 96.

**Does recording more flights give me more chances?**
No. Each eligible trial month gives one entry. Reaching 80% is what counts.

**What is the prize?**
The planned prize is the experimental vehicle, at no cost to the winner, under the trial terms published before each draw cycle.

**How is the winner chosen?**
By a cryptographically secure random draw, held in the presence of an independent observer, with two reserve winners.

**Can SkyShift employees win?**
No. Employees, advisory-circle members, the independent observer and their immediate families are not eligible.

**How will I know if I won?**
By email, within 14 days of the draw. You must confirm within 30 days.

## The vehicles

**What is a SkyCar?**
SkyShift's concept for a dual-mode vehicle: a compact electric car on the road that becomes an eVTOL aircraft at a designated take-off point (SkyPad).

**Do the cars in the catalog really fly?**
No. The catalog images are edited pictures of road cars with added propellers. They illustrate the idea. The road cars shown do not fly.

**Is SkyShift connected to the car manufacturers in the catalog?**
No. SkyShift has no connection to them, and they have not approved or endorsed SkyShift.

**How many models are in the catalog?**
22.

**What do "Verified" and "Illustrative" mean?**
"Verified" (manufacturer specification) means the value comes from an official manufacturer document. "Illustrative" means it is SkyShift sample data, not a manufacturer specification.

**How fast does a SkyCar fly?**
The design target for the SkyCar Five is a cruise speed of 140 km/h. This is a design target, not a measured value.

**How far can a SkyCar fly?**
Design target: up to 70 km on a single charge, plus a mandatory 20-minute energy reserve.

**How many people does a SkyCar carry?**
The SkyCar Five is designed for 4 passengers plus a safety pilot. The SkyCar Two is designed for 1 passenger plus a safety pilot.

**Will I have to fly it myself?**
No. During the trial, flights are carried out by a trained SkyShift safety pilot, with monitoring from the flight-supervision centre.

**Can a SkyCar take off from the street?**
No. Take-off and landing take place only at approved SkyPads.

**Is it electric?**
Yes. Fully electric in both road and air modes, with zero local emissions.

**How noisy is it?**
Design target: 65 dBA or less at 100 m during cruise.

## Safety

**Is the SkyCar safe?**
The SkyCar is designed to the strictest relevant category of international standards: the Enhanced category of EASA's SC-VTOL, with a target of no more than one catastrophic failure in a billion flight hours, and no single failure allowed to be catastrophic. It is still a concept and is not yet certified.

**What happens if a rotor fails?**
The design has eight rotors, and the SkyCar is designed to continue safe flight and landing after losing any one of them.

**Is there a parachute?**
Yes. The design includes a whole-vehicle ballistic recovery parachute for extreme emergencies.

**What about bad weather?**
There are strict weather limits: no flights in thunderstorms or icing, and wind limits of 25 knots steady and 30 knots in gusts. In the first stage of the pilot, flights take place in daytime visual conditions only.

**Who can stop a flight?**
The safety pilot, the flight-supervision centre, and the Chief Safety and Airworthiness Officer, who has the authority to stop any activity.

**How do I report a safety concern?**
Write to safety@skyshift.demo. Reports are handled confidentially.

## The platform and privacy

**What technology is SkyShift built with?**
A .NET 8 (ASP.NET Core) server, a React and TypeScript web application, and a PostgreSQL database.

**Is my password safe?**
Passwords are stored only as secure hashes. SkyShift never stores or asks for your password in readable form.

**Do you sell my data?**
No.

**Which languages are supported?**
Hebrew and English.

**How can I try the platform?**
Register with your email address in the SkyShift demonstration platform. Registration signs you in immediately.
