## Part A: Glossary

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **SkyShift** | The venture and the technical name of its platform |
| **עולים לאוויר ("Olim La'Avir")** | SkyShift's Hebrew business name, meaning "taking to the air" |
| **Participant (User)** | A person registered in the SkyShift platform |
| **Manager** | A member of the SkyShift operations team with management permissions |
| **Trial month** | The one-month period of a vehicle assignment, from the moment of selection to exactly one month later, both ends included |
| **Vehicle assignment** | The record linking a participant to a vehicle for a trial month |
| **Average daily trips** | The whole number a participant declares at registration |
| **Monthly target (expected monthly trips)** | Average daily trips × 30 |
| **Completed flight** | A flight accepted by the platform within the active trial month |
| **Completion percentage** | Completed flights × 100 ÷ monthly target, rounded down |
| **Eligibility threshold** | 80% of the monthly target |
| **Draw cycle** | A published period at the end of which a draw is held among eligible participants |
| **Independent observer** | A person who is not a SkyShift employee and supervises the draw |
| **Experimental vehicle / personal experimental vehicle** | A fleet vehicle designated for personal trial use, from which the lottery prize is planned to come |
| **Catalog** | The list of 22 vehicle models from which fleet vehicles are created |
| **Fleet** | The actual vehicles available for participants to select |
| **Verified (Manufacturer specification)** | A value taken from an official manufacturer document |
| **Illustrative** | SkyShift sample data, not a manufacturer specification |
| **Visual inspiration** | A catalog model whose exact identity is not verified |
| **Vehicle statuses** | Available, In use, Maintenance, Unavailable, Retired |
| **SkyCar** | SkyShift's dual-mode road and air vehicle concept |
| **SkyCar Five** | The flagship concept: 4 passengers plus a safety pilot |
| **SkyCar Two** | A lighter concept: 1 passenger plus a safety pilot |
| **Road mode** | The SkyCar operating as an electric car |
| **Air mode** | The SkyCar operating as an eVTOL aircraft |
| **SkyPad** | A designated, approved site for take-off, landing, conversion and charging |
| **Conversion** | Switching between road mode and air mode, only at a SkyPad |
| **Safety pilot** | A trained, qualified SkyShift pilot who carries out every pilot-stage flight |
| **Flight-supervision centre** | The SkyShift centre that monitors every flight in real time |
| **eVTOL** | Electric vertical take-off and landing aircraft |
| **Powered-lift** | The FAA category of aircraft to which most eVTOL aircraft belong |
| **SC-VTOL** | EASA's Special Condition for small-category VTOL aircraft (July 2019) |
| **Category Enhanced** | The stricter SC-VTOL category, for operations over congested areas or commercial passenger transport |
| **10⁻⁹ per flight hour** | One in a billion: the maximum probability target for catastrophic failure conditions in Category Enhanced |
| **SSF** | SkyShift Safety Framework |
| **Geofencing** | A flight-control function that prevents the aircraft from leaving its approved area |
| **Ballistic recovery parachute** | A parachute that can lower the entire vehicle in an extreme emergency |
| **CAAI** | Civil Aviation Authority of Israel |
| **INDI** | Israel National Drone Initiative (SkyShift is not part of it) |
| **Demonstration phase** | The current stage: the platform is live, but there are no real flights and no active draw |

## Part B: Roadmap

> All dates beyond October 2026 are **plans**, not commitments. Every flight-related stage depends on regulatory approvals and on meeting the SkyShift Safety Framework.

### Stage 1: Foundations (completed)

**March 2025 – June 2026**
- Founding of SkyShift.
- Research phase: participant survey, interviews, regulatory review.
- Definition of the SkyCar concept.
- First version of the SkyShift Safety Framework.
- Approval of the trial program rules.

### Stage 2: Demonstration platform (current)

**September 2026 – early 2027**
- SkyShift platform: participant workspace and manager workspace. *(Completed.)*
- Vehicle catalog of 22 models with accuracy labels. *(Completed.)*
- PostgreSQL support and public deployment. *(Completed.)*
- **SkyShift assistant bot** that answers questions from this knowledge base. *(In development.)*
- Automatic return of vehicles to the available pool at the end of a trial. *(Planned.)*
- Password reset and profile editing. *(Planned.)*
- Personal flight history for participants. *(Planned.)*

### Stage 3: Engineering and approvals (planned)

**2027**
- Detailed design of the SkyCar Five.
- Ground testing of propulsion, batteries and flight-control systems.
- Discussions with the relevant aviation authorities on the certification and operational basis.
- Selection of sites for the first SkyPads.
- Safety-pilot training programme.

### Stage 4: Closed pilot (planned)

**Subject to approvals**
- A small number of SkyCars and SkyPads in a defined area in central Israel.
- A limited group of participants.
- Flights verified automatically from vehicle data.
- Flight booking and SkyPad scheduling in the platform.
- The first draw cycle, under the published lottery regulations.

### Stage 5: Learning and expansion (vision)

- Publication of aggregated, anonymous results of the pilot.
- Gradual expansion of the SkyPad network and corridors.
- Study of the long-term possibility of more autonomous operations, subject to separate regulatory approval.

## Part C: What will not change

Whatever the roadmap brings, three things stay fixed:

1. **No flight without full safety and regulatory approval.**
2. **Clear statements of status:** demonstration, illustration and design target are always labelled as such.
3. **The same rules for everyone**, calculated by the system and published in advance.
